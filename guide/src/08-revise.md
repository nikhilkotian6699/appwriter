# Revising

Click **Revise**. The lead writer receives the chapter, the story bible and the
notes you accepted with their final wording, and applies those notes and
nothing else. It is told to keep your voice, rhythm, vocabulary and paragraphs
everywhere it was not asked to change anything. You see its words arrive in the
**Lead writer** card.

When it finishes, the review opens.

```figure
revision-dialog
```

- Every change is shown twice, **before** on the left and **after** on the
  right (A), with the removed words struck through in red and the added words
  in green. The grey words around them are context.
- Each change has a tick box (B). All are ticked to begin with.
- **Accept all** (C) applies every change. **Accept selected** applies only the
  ticked ones. **Discard** (D) throws the whole revision away; the chapter is
  untouched.

Before anything is applied, the app snapshots the chapter as it was, labelled
*Before revision*, so you can always go back through **Versions**.

Two things to know:

- Changes are applied to the chapter *as it was when the revision was
  proposed*. If you edit the chapter in between, the revision becomes **stale**
  and can no longer be applied; discard it and ask for a new one.
- Only the passages behind your accepted notes change. The lead writer is not
  asked to improve anything else, and the diff shows you whether it obeyed.
