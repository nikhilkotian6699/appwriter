# Co-writing and comparing drafts

Workflow 2 is for getting words onto the page: a writer drafts something for
you, in its own voice, with the story bible as context. You stay in control:
nothing goes into the chapter until you put it there.

## Asking for a draft

1. In the editor, either **select a passage** you want reworked, or put the
   cursor where new text should go.
2. Click **Co-write**.

```figure
cowrite-dialog
```

3. Pick a writer (A). Only writers with the co-writer role appear.
4. Say what you want (B). The buttons underneath fill in common requests:
   *Draft this scene*, *Continue from here*, *Rewrite this dialogue*, *Tighten
   this passage*, *Give me 3 alternative openings*. You can write anything.
5. Add **scene notes** if you like (C): what happens, who is there, what the
   reader should feel.
6. Click **Ask for a draft** (D).

With a passage selected, the writer proposes text to take its place. With just
a cursor, it continues from there, reading the text before and after.

## Deciding what to do with it

```figure
drafts
```

The draft streams into the Guild panel (A). When it is done:

- **Insert at cursor** (B) puts it where your cursor is.
- **Replace selection** (C) swaps it in for the passage you had selected.
- **Discard** (D) sets it aside; **Take back** brings a discarded draft back.

Inserting goes through the editor like typing, so **Undo** in the toolbar
reverses it, and the autosave keeps the version before.

## Comparing writers

Tick two or three writers in the Co-write dialog and the button becomes
**Compare**. The same request goes to each, their drafts arrive side by side,
and each has its own Insert, Replace and Discard. One writer failing does not
spoil the others.
