# History, cost and stats

## History

Click **History** on a chapter to see everything the Guild has done on it,
newest first.

```figure
history
```

- The totals at the top (A) count the runs, their cost and their tokens.
- The chips (B) filter by kind: critique, revision, bible update, co-write,
  compare, writer test.
- Each row (C) says what happened and what came of it, for instance *3 critics ·
  4 issues · 1 accepted · 1 rejected* or *2 changes proposed · 1 applied*.
  Click a row to see every request it made to the gateway, with the writer,
  tokens, cost and time of each.

## About cost

Every request's cost comes from the gateway. Streamed replies, which is most of
them, do not carry a cost, so the app estimates it from the number of tokens
and the gateway's price list, and marks it **est.** wherever it is shown. Treat
those numbers as close, not exact.

## Writer stats

**Stats** in the top bar shows how useful each writer has been to you.

```figure
stats
```

- Pick a period and a project (A).
- For each writer (B): how many of the editor-in-chief's notes cited it, how
  many of those you accepted or rejected, and the resulting acceptance rate; how
  many drafts it wrote and how many you used; what it cost, by kind of run.
- A note on the editor-in-chief's list counts for every critic who raised it,
  because the editor merges several critics' notes into one.

Use it to decide which writers to keep convening and which to retire.
