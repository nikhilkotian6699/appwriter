# The editor-in-chief's list and your decisions

```figure
editor-list
```

Each item on the list (A) has:

- a **severity**: high, medium or low;
- the **passage** it is about, in italics: click it (B) to see it highlighted in
  your chapter;
- the **problem** and a suggested **fix**;
- **Raised by** (C): which critics raised it. One note often merges several
  critics' notes.

Under each item are your choices (D):

- **Accept**: this change should be made.
- **Edit fix**: you agree with the problem but want the fix in your own words.
  Type it and click **Save and accept**. Your wording is what the lead writer
  will follow.
- **Reject**: leave the passage as it is.
- **Undo** takes any decision back.

The header counts what you have decided (E). Nothing changes in the chapter
yet; deciding only marks the notes. When you are ready, click **Revise** (F).
It is active once at least one note is accepted.

If you edit the chapter after a critique, the panel warns you that it is
stale; the notes may no longer point at text that still exists. You can still
decide on them, and the revision skips any passage it can no longer find.
