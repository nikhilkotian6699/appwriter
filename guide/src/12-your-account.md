# Your account

Click your name at the top right to open the Account page.

```figure
account
```

- **Display name** (A) is what the top bar shows instead of your username.
- **Change password** (B): type your current password, then the new one twice.
  It needs at least 8 characters. Changing it signs you out of every other
  browser; the one you are using stays signed in.

Your **Settings** page (top bar) has two numbers that affect how the Guild
reads: the *scene token limit*, above which a chapter is split into scenes
before the writers read it, and how often the editor keeps an autosave
snapshot. The defaults are fine to start with.
