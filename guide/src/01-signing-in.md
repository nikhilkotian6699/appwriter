# Signing in

Open the address your admin gave you. You will see the sign-in page.

```figure
login
```

1. Type your **username** (A). Usernames are all lower-case.
2. Type your **password** (B).
3. Click **Sign in** (C).

If you mistype the password five times in a row, the app makes you wait a
minute before you can try again, then longer each time, up to a quarter of an
hour. This protects your account; it is not broken. Wait, then try again.

If you forgot your password, ask your admin. They can set a new one for you on
their Users page; you will be signed out everywhere and can sign in with the new
one. Then change it to something only you know on your
[Account page](#your-account).

To sign out, click **Sign out** at the top right. Signing out only affects the
browser you are using.

> Everything you make belongs to your account alone. Other people with accounts
> on the same Writers' Guild cannot see your projects, chapters or story bible,
> and neither can the admin.
