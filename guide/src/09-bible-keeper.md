# Keeping the story bible

After you apply a revision, the bible keeper reads the chapter as it now
stands, together with the bible and the list of what changed, and proposes
updates to the bible: a new entry, a change to an existing one, or a deletion,
each with one sentence saying why.

```figure
bible-proposals
```

The proposals appear in the Guild panel right after the revision and also at
the top of the story bible page, where they wait until you decide.

- **Approve** (A) writes the change into the bible.
- **Edit and approve** (B) lets you fix the title or any field first, then
  writes it.
- **Reject** (C) drops it.

For a change to an existing entry, the fields show the current value struck
through and the new value beside it, so you can see exactly what would change.
Nothing is written to the bible until you approve it.

You can also ask the keeper to look at a chapter by hand from the chapter's
Guild panel after a revision, if you want a second look.
