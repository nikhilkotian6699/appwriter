# Writing a chapter

Open a chapter from the project page and you are in the editor.

```figure
chapter-editor
```

- Write in the big white area (A). The toolbar (B) has bold, italic, headings,
  lists, a block quote, and a **scene break** (the row of three stars), which
  also tells the Guild where one scene ends and the next begins.
- The chapter saves itself as you type. The status next to the word count (C)
  says *Saving…*, then *Saved*. If it says *Unsaved changes* for long, or
  *Could not save*, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
- The chapter title is the line at the top (D); click it to rename.

## Versions

The app keeps snapshots of your text: one every few minutes while you write,
one before every revision or restore, and any you take yourself. Click
**Versions** (E) to see them.

- **Snapshot** with a label saves the text as it is now, for instance *before I
  cut the ending*.
- **View** shows an old version without changing anything.
- **Restore** puts an old version back. The text you had is snapshotted first,
  so a restore can itself be undone.

## The buttons that call the Guild

- **Convene the Guild** (F) asks the critics to read the chapter. See
  [Convening the Guild](#convening-the-guild).
- **Co-write** (G) asks one writer to draft something for you. See
  [Co-writing](#co-writing-and-comparing-drafts).
- **History** (H) lists everything the Guild has done on this chapter, with
  what it cost. See [History, cost and stats](#history-cost-and-stats).
- **Guild** (I) opens or hides the panel where the writers' work appears.
