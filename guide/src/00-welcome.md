# Welcome to the Writers' Guild

The Writers' Guild is a place to write a novel with help. You write the
chapters. A panel of AI writers, each with a distinct voice, reads them and
tells you what works and what does not. An editor-in-chief gathers their notes
into one list. You decide, note by note, what to accept. A lead writer makes
only the changes you accepted, and you approve every change before it lands in
your text. A bible keeper watches that the facts of your story stay straight.

Nothing changes in your manuscript unless you say so. That is the one rule the
whole app is built around.

This guide is for people who have never done any of this before, including
people who have never written a story. It walks through everything from signing
in to a revised chapter, screen by screen. You can read it top to bottom, or jump
to the part you need from the list on the left.

## What you need

- Your username and password. The person who set up the Writers' Guild for you
  (the *admin*) gives you both. You can change the password afterwards.
- A browser. Nothing to install.
- Something to write about. If you have nothing yet, there is a
  [sample chapter](#a-sample-chapter-to-paste) in this guide to paste in and
  play with.

## How the pieces fit together

| You | The Guild |
|---|---|
| Write a chapter in the editor. | Keeps every version, so nothing is ever lost. |
| Click **Convene the Guild**. | The critics read the chapter and the story bible, each in their own voice. |
| Read the editor-in-chief's list. | The notes are merged into one prioritized list, each one pointing at an exact passage. |
| Accept, reject, or edit each note. | Only what you accept goes further. |
| Click **Revise**. | The lead writer applies the accepted notes and shows you every change side by side. |
| Accept all, some, or none of the changes. | The chapter is updated, and the version before is kept. |
| Approve or reject the bible keeper's proposals. | The story bible stays true to the text. |

The rest of this guide takes these one at a time.
